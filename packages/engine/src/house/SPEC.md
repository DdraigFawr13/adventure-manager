# The guild house (D-168–D-193)

What rooms cost to build, raise and keep, how long building takes, how barracks beds cap the roster (v0), room effects (v1) and incidents (v2). The library's study places and the other rooms' effects wait for the systems they feed. Every number is a starting assumption in content (`packages/content/src/base/house.ts`), tuned with `tools/career-sim.ts`.

## Space

- The **hall** is a stack of floors, each 3 cells wide (D-169). Floor 0 is the ground floor; floors 1, 2… are storeys; floors −1, −2… are cellars. The **compound** has two sides, west and east, each a row of plots, plot 0 next to the hall (D-180, D-183).
- A cell or plot is empty or holds one cell of a **room kind** at a level from 1 to the kind's top level (3 at launch, D-172). The **study** is a fixed cell: it can't be built, upgraded or removed, and it costs no upkeep.
- A **room** is a run of side-by-side cells of the same kind on one floor or one compound side; a run is never longer than the kind's `maxCells` (1–3). The room's level is the lowest level among its cells.
- Each kind says where it may go (D-176, D-180): `hall` or `compound`, and `belowGround` (cellars only) for the strongroom.
- A new guild starts with the content's starting layout (floors, compound sides and cells already built). The founding roster may not exceed the starting beds.

## Building

All building is by command, paid in full when it starts (`money.changed`, reason `building`), refused if the chest can't pay, and finished at the first week start on or after its due week. A cell or plot under work can't take another command until it finishes. Commands:

- `house.build` (place, kind): a new cell of that kind in an empty cell or plot. Next to a room of the same kind, the new cell joins it at that room's level and costs the kind's cell cost summed over levels 1 to that level; otherwise it is level 1.
- `house.upgrade` (place of any cell of a room): raises every cell of the room that is at the room's level by one, at that level's cost per cell.
- `house.extend` (direction): `up` adds an empty floor on top, `down` digs an empty cellar below the lowest floor, `west` or `east` adds an empty plot at the far end of that side, up to the content's maximum. Each step costs more than the last: the content's base plus its step for each one already added (D-172, D-183).
- A second `house.extend` in a direction already under work is refused. A room with any cell under work (a new cell joining it, or an upgrade) can't be joined or upgraded until that work finishes, so a cell never lands at a level that is about to change.
- Only the extensions rise in price (each storey, cellar or wall push costs more than the last); a room's cells cost the same on every floor. The owner confirmed this reading of D-172 (D-185).
- Work time is the content's weeks for the job; a job started in winter takes `winterWeeksBp` of that, rounded up (best done in winter, D-067, D-169; the size of the saving is an assumption).
- Events: `house.started` when paid, `house.finished` when done. Neither holds state objects.

## Running the house

- Each week start, every built cell costs its kind's upkeep at its level (`money.changed`, reason `rooms`), paid with the wages (D-177). The study costs nothing.
- **Beds** (D-170, D-174): the barracks' cells give beds by level. `recruit.sign` is refused while the guild has as many members as beds, so no one is signed while anyone lodges at the inn (D-182).
- **Lodgers** (D-182, D-190): members beyond the beds (after a fire burns barracks beds) lodge at the inn. Each week start, after room upkeep, the guild pays `incidents.innFeeCopper` per lodger (`money.changed`, reason `inn`), into debt if need be. Lodgers are worked out each week (members less beds), so they need no state; rebuilding beds ends the fee. The fee is one number until the town's inn exists in the engine (D-171).
- Without house rules in content nothing changes: no house state, no upkeep, no bed cap (the existing simulators are unchanged).

## Room effects (v1, D-170, D-186)

- **Infirmary beds:** each infirmary cell gives sickbeds by level. A member who comes home with a lasting injury takes a free sickbed if there is one and keeps it until fit again (`outUntilDay`), or until they die or leave. Their injury days are scaled as now by whoever runs recovery (staff/SPEC.md). With no free sickbed the injury days are multiplied by `noSickbedBp` (20,000: twice as long, D-186). Sickbeds go first come, first served as members come home; the head's other policies (worst wounds first, veterans first) come with the room page. Events: `member.laid-up` gains whether the member had a sickbed.
- **Training at home (D-187):** each week start, every member who is at home and fit (`isFree`) may grow a point in one attribute below their potential, picked at random. The base chance by age is the content's `homeTraining` (young / prime; none past the prime, as with quest growth), scaled by whoever runs training (staff/SPEC.md), then by `placedBp` (15,000) for a member with a yard place or `unplacedBp` (7,500) without one. Each training-yard cell gives places by level. Places go to the members at home and fit, youngest first, ties by id, worked out each week, so they need no state (the head's default policy, D-170). Growth after quests is unchanged. Stream `house.training`; events `attribute.changed` with reason `training`.
- The heads: the existing staff areas stand for the two rooms' heads, recovery for the infirmary and training for the yard (D-170). The library's study places and the other rooms' effects wait for the systems they feed.
- Without house rules none of this applies: injuries and growth run as before.

## Incidents (v2, D-173, D-191, D-192)

With `incidents` in the house rules, the house has a few incidents a year, each a letter with a choice. Each kind is a content entry (`packages/content/src/base/house.ts`); the engine knows three effects: `fire`, `theft` and `sickness`. Every number is an assumption tuned with the simulator toward 1-2 incidents a year.

- **Chance** (D-191): each week start, after upkeep, inn fees and training, if no letter stands, the house rolls for each kind in content order, then for each eligible target in place order (hall floors low to high, cells left to right, then west plots, then east plots), on stream `house.incidents`. The first strike ends the rolls for the week, so at most one incident strikes a week. A target's chance is the kind's `weeklyBp`, times `levelBp` at the room's level, times `debtBp` while the chest is below zero, times `fullBp` when the target is full; with the multipliers in basis points, rounded down at each step.
- **Targets:** `fire` and `sickness` strike one finished cell of the kind's `roomKindIds` that is not under work. `sickness` strikes only a crowded barracks: members at least `crowdedBp` of the beds (D-206; 3/4 in the base content), and a full one at the `fullBp` multiplier. `theft` strikes the chest, only when the share taken would be at least 1 copper; its level is the strongroom's (the best strongroom cell; `noRoomBp` stands in for `levelBp` when there is none). "Full" is members at or above beds for a barracks, every sickbed held for an infirmary, and members at home and fit at or above the places for a training yard; other kinds are never full.
- **The letter:** a strike emits `incident.struck` (kind, effect, place or null, the paid answer's fee) and a must stop `incident`, and the letter stands (`house.letter`) until it is answered or the next week start. `incident.answer` (day today, `paid` or `free`) answers it; an unanswered letter takes `free` at the next week start, before that week's rolls (`byDefault: true`). Either way `incident.settled` reports the kind, the answer and what happened. The paid answer is refused if the chest can't pay its fee, except the fire watch (D-193); its fee is `money.changed`, reason `incident`.
- **Fire:** free (let it burn): the struck cell drops a level; a level-1 cell burns out (empty). A burnt barracks cell loses its beds, so lodgers may follow. Paid (the fire watch): no damage. While a letter stands, its cell is held: no build, upgrade or repair can start on it, so no work erases the damage.
- **Repairs (D-193):** a fire's damage leaves a scar: the place, its kind and its level before the fire (a second fire on a scarred place keeps the first scar's level). `house.repair` (place) restores a scarred place to that level: `incidents.repairBp` of the cell costs of the levels restored, the weeks of the top level restored (winter as usual), and it may take the chest below zero. It is refused if the place is under work or holds another kind, or if the restored cell would make its run longer than the kind's `maxCells`; builds and upgrades count cells under repair as part of their run. A repaired cell comes back at its old level even if its run has been raised since; raising it again is an upgrade. Building another kind on a burnt-out place clears its scar. Events as for other work (`house.started`, `house.finished`, job `repair`); a repair is paid with `money.changed`, reason `repair`. Paying the fire watch may also take the chest below zero; the other paid answers still need the coin.
- **Theft:** at the strike, thieves take `shareBp` of the chest at the strongroom's level (`noRoomBp` share when none), rounded down (`money.changed`, reason `theft`). Free (write it off): nothing more. Paid (thief-takers): the fee, then a roll at `recoverBp` on stream `house.incidents`; on success the stolen coin comes back (reason `theft`).
- **Sickness:** `count` members at home and fit (drawn on stream `house.incidents`, fewer if fewer are home) fall ill: out for `freeDays` (quarantine) or `paidDays` (a healer), and a sick member who takes a free sickbed (held as for injuries, D-186) is out for that times `sickbedBp`, at least 1 day. Each sick member emits `member.fell-ill` (days, sickbed); `member.recovered` follows as for injuries. Only members at home and fit are drawn.
- **Simulator:** repairs fire damage first (barracks at once, into debt; other rooms under the build reserve), answers every letter with the default (D-192), and reports incidents a year by kind, the share of fires that burn barracks beds, weeks with lodgers, inn fees and theft as shares of spending, and dismissals against the house-on run without incidents.
- Without `incidents` in the house rules, nothing here applies (no rolls, no lodgers' fee, no draws on the stream).

## What the simulator measures (D-172)

With the house on, the career simulator follows the content's `fullHousePlan`, step by step toward a reference **full house** (every kind at level 3), one job at a time, starting the next step at a week start when the chest after paying stays above a reserve of 24 weeks of spending (wages, running cost and upkeep; 12 until D-205, when guilds building on a 12-week reserve fell into debt in year 1). It reports the year the first extra room is started and finished, the year the full house is finished, the full house's total cost in years of the middle tier's median surplus, and room upkeep as a share of spending. Targets: the first extra room within the first season; the full house costing about 8–12 years of a mid guild's surplus.
