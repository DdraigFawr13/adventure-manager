# Delve (gate 1)

Led fights and delves, beside resolution v0 (D-229). Full rules: `docs/specs/gate1-one-delve.md`, Design "Engine"; numbers in `packages/content/src/gate1/`; screens D-231 and D-232. S1 built one fight; S2 adds rooms, Auto, retreat and loot.

- **State.** `GameState.delve: DelveRun | null`. The party's `fighters` carry health between rooms; `fight` holds the foes, the kept `order`, `turn`, who has `acted` this round, the foes' `intents` and the round marks (Ward on the fight; Spark, Guard and "hit this round" on fighters).
- **Rolls.** Only named streams via `streamFor`: `delve.fight` (hits, damage, bleed, death, injury, retreat), `delve.rooms` (layout, choice checks, scout), `delve.auto` (Auto's good-choice roll), `delve.loot` (parts). Views never call them; the page saves after every command, so a reload replays the same rolls.
- **Order.** Set at the fight's start by speed, ties by id. Moves last: knockback one place later, Hamstring to last, Haste right after the wizard. The next actor is the first fighter after the current one who has not acted this round; anyone passed while not up counts as passed, so a raised ally acts again from the next round, and a hasted ally who already acted acts there from the next round.
- **Commands.** `delve.start` (`rooms.ts`) lays out the rooms on `delve.rooms` and enters the first: a site with an entry and a boss gets entry, fight, fight, maybe a sixth room (a choice room or a fourth fight at equal odds), fight, boss; S1's sample site keeps its one room. `fight.act` and `fight.step` (Auto, `auto.ts`) take the current adventurer's turn, then run the foe turns and return at the next adventurer's turn or the fight's end. `delve.choose`, `delve.scout`, `delve.rows` and `delve.next` work between rooms (phase `breather`, also after a choice room); `fight.retreat` (`retreat.ts`) ends the delve. `refusal` is the one validity check for actions, shared with `fightView` and Auto.
- **Rooms.** Choice checks are v0's (base + step × (attribute − difficulty by danger)); the entry's first way tests the approach's attribute. Success either way through the entry sets off guard for the first fight (D-232); a failed `alert` way, or a heard scout, alerts the next fight and a following choice room passes it on; off guard and alerted cancel; both clear when a fight starts. Alerted foes take their turns before any adventurer in round 1. Scout ahead needs a standing member of the scout class, once per gap; a seen scout (`seen`) shows the next room and allows `delve.rows`.
- **Breather and loot.** A won fight drops each Down foe's part on `delve.loot` (the boss always); then, unless it was the boss room or the last room, the breather: Down members rise to the breather health, Dying ones become Down at 0 and stay Down. Nothing refills.
- **Auto.** The class's rules in order; each decision rolls the good-choice chance on `delve.auto`; a miss takes the plain attack on the first reachable foe by order. Save-for-boss skips skill rules outside the boss room except Mend on a Down or Dying ally. Readings the spec left open: a draught goes to a Dying ally before the `always` rule (Auto's only pack use); `once` rules are kept in `fight.autoOnce`.
- **Retreat.** The standing member with the best Wits checks against the danger; beats = base, + carrying anyone Down or Dying, + a failed check. Each beat every standing foe strikes once by its target rule (intents dropped), then the round-end rolls. The delve ends `retreated` if anyone stands, else `routed`.
- **Numbers.** `hitChance` and `damageRange` (in `actions.ts`) are the only hit and damage rules; `fight.act` and `previewAction` both call them. Heals, Guard, Ward, Haste, swapping rows and items never miss.
- **Readings the spec left open (S1):** the cleric's plain attack heals the most hurt standing ally 1 whether or not it hits; a firebomb's damage is reduced by each foe's armour (at least 1) and adds no weapon rarity; Cleave rolls to hit each of its two targets; a guarded ally's hits are rolled against the warrior; Spark marks, knockback and Hamstring's move happen only on a hit; the fight also ends at once when no adventurer or no foe stands.
- **End.** At the round's end: bleed rolls for the Down, death rolls for those Dying before them, marks clear (`round.ended`), then the flee check (more than half Down; boss fights end on the boss). The delve ends won after the boss room (or S1's one room), routed when no one stands, or retreated: `delve.ended` with each member's wound step, injury roll and death, copied to `run.ends`.
