Guildmaster,
We have walked out of The Hollow Barrow, and the place is quiet now, every one of us alive.
We came up against the restless warden, and it was brief, which suited us, after they had heard us coming.
In the fight, Wenna Rushmere struck home with 3 blows and barely broke a sweat, to hear them tell it.
While it was still going, Ysolde Crowe patched one of us up more than once, and scolded the patient while doing it.
To the business end of it, the reeve has the fee counted out for you, and we will need time.
Rook Wherry

---

To the guildmaster,
We have come back out of Cray's Mill Cellar, and you can mark it paid, fresh enough to grumble.
We met cellar rats, and it was over quickly, and we finished it.
When it mattered, Wenna Rushmere took a blow meant for someone else, and only grumbled about it once.
The honours go to Ysolde Crowe, who got in 3 blows, more than the rest of us together, or near enough.
Since you will ask, the reeve paid on the spot, which never happens, and we are ready for the next.
Yours, Isolt Holloway

---

Guildmaster,
We backed out of The Drowned Chapel when it turned against us.
As it fell out, we did not come within a stone's throw of the chapel keeper, and the rest of this letter says why.
Petronel Ferriby held on to Isolt Holloway with both hands and a deal of swearing.
With no time to spare, Tobin Marsh patched one of us up more than once, steady as a mill wheel.
Now, the fee: the contract is lost, and the fee with it, and we will need time.
In haste, Isolt Holloway

---

For the guildmaster's hand,
We lost at The Gallows Spire, and that is the plain truth of it, slower than we went.
We stood against the gallows lord, and it was a proper fight; someone raised the alarm, and it showed.
When it came to it, Tobin Marsh would not let go of Petronel Ferriby with both hands and a deal of swearing.
Under the worst of it, Petronel Ferriby got a hurt one standing and still found time to pray.
I should say that Tobin Marsh is hurt in a way that will last, and is cross about it.
Now, the fee: the contract is lost, and the fee with it; we need rest before anything else.
Isolt Holloway, for the party

---

For the guildmaster's hand,
We walked away from The Hollow Barrow on the leader's word, nobody lost.
Down at the heart of it we found the restless warden, and it was brief, which suited us.
With no time to spare, Tobin Marsh patched one of us up again and again and still found time to pray.
To the business end of it, no pay, and no one to ask for it, and we will need time.
In haste, Simkin Bellows

---

For the guildmaster's hand,
We are done with Cray's Mill Cellar with nothing left there to trouble anyone, sore in every joint.
We had it out with cellar rats, and it was quick work, and we finished it.
Between blows, Ysolde Crowe got a hurt one standing, steady as a mill wheel.
Wenna Rushmere is hurt in a way that will last, and knows it.
Now, the fee: they paid what was owed without haggling, which surprised us; most of us will mend.
Rook Wherry, for the party
