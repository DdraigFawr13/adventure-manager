Guildmaster,
We are back from The Hollow Barrow, and the place is quiet now, nobody lost.
We found the restless warden, and it did not last, after they had heard us coming.
In the fight, Wenna Rushmere found the mark with 3 blows, and is already telling it in the tavern.
With no time to spare, Ysolde Crowe got a hurt one standing more than once, which kept the rest of us in it.
On the matter of pay, the fee will be at the hall before we are; most of us will mend.
Rook Wherry

---

For the guildmaster's hand,
We are done with Cray's Mill Cellar with nothing left there to trouble anyone, without a scratch worth the name.
We stood against cellar rats, and it was done before anyone thought to be afraid, and it went our way.
Without a word, Wenna Rushmere stood in the way of a blow, without being asked.
The honours go to Ysolde Crowe, who put in 3 blows and barely broke a sweat, to hear her tell it.
About the money, the fee is good, and so is the loot, and we would take another like it.
Isolt Holloway, for the party

---

Guildmaster,
We gave up on The Drowned Chapel before the place kept us, bruised but all here.
We never reached the chapel keeper; the road did for us first.
Were it not for Petronel Ferriby, Isolt Holloway would not have seen another morning.
Between blows, Tobin Marsh closed a wound that had no business closing 2 times, steady as a mill wheel.
About the money, the contract is lost, and the fee with it, and we will need time.
Isolt Holloway, for the party

---

For the guildmaster's hand,
We were thrown out of The Gallows Spire and are home only because they let us go, slower than we went.
Down at the heart of it we found the gallows lord, and it was ugly, with them ready and us not.
But for Tobin Marsh, Petronel Ferriby would be buried there, and she knows it.
With no time to spare, Petronel Ferriby put a hurt comrade back in the line, muttering the whole time.
Worse news, Tobin Marsh is hurt in a way that will last; the surgeon will say how badly.
Now, the fee: do not wait on any money from this, and the infirmary will be busy.
In haste, Isolt Holloway

---

Guildmaster, from the road home,
We pulled out of The Hollow Barrow before it finished us.
We had it out at last with the restless warden, and it did not last, with no chance to set ourselves.
In the thick of it, Tobin Marsh set someone back on their feet 4 times, steady as a mill wheel.
There is nothing owed to us; send the surgeon to meet us.
Simkin Bellows, for the party

---

Guildmaster, from the road home,
We are done with Cray's Mill Cellar, and it is settled, nobody lost.
We had it out with cellar rats, and it did not last, and we came out on top.
Ysolde Crowe put a hurt comrade back in the line, muttering the whole time.
Tell the infirmary that Wenna Rushmere will be a long time mending, and is taking it better than I would.
The reeve has the fee counted out for you; we need rest before anything else.
Rook Wherry, for the party
