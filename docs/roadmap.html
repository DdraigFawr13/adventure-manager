The plan, section by section

Part A is the game's design; Part B is how it gets made. Decision numbers point into docs/DECISIONS.md.

  • Part A · Game design
  • 1Goals and target playerDoneManagement-sim veterans first; a season takes about 2–2.5 h; design goals and success targets. D-063–065
  • 2Core loop and pacingDoneA season is one year of 48 weeks; about 80 stops a year. D-066–068
  • 3Systems mapDone14 launch systems; ranking by renown earned this year. D-069–070
  • 4Economy and balanceDoneAbout 2 deaths a year in a mid guild; money tight early; 3 tiers of 6 guilds. D-071–073
  • 5ProgressionDone10 class levels (20 later); retirees become staff; succession. D-074–075
  • 6Onboarding, UX and accessibilityDonePrologue, staff-opened ledgers, clerk's notes; clear print; screen rules. D-076–078
  • 7Launch content listDoneFive ancestries; 36 monster kinds, 60 key moments, about 6,000 lines. D-079–081
  • 8Making consequences visibleDoneIn-world letters with a Trace; numbered inspector histories. D-082–083
  • 9Risks and prototypesDoneNine risks with tests; ledger minimum 15 px with lining figures; watched quest first. D-084–086
  • 10Playtest and metrics planDonePlaytest logs only, release sends nothing; owner, then 6–10, 30–50, demo, 200+ beta. D-088–089
