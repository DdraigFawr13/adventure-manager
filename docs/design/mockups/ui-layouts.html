StrengthsThe most atmospheric: every screen is an object on your desk, and the map sits at the centre of the guild's life. Strong first impression in trailers and the demo.
WeaknessesEvery trip costs an extra step (back to the desk, then pick an object). Twelve ledgers don't fit on a desk, so some systems get buried. FM-scale play gets slow without heavy keyboard shortcuts.

StrengthsThe home screen is the desk: the map in the middle with your parties and rivals as miniatures, the letter tray always open on the right, and this week's parties along the bottom. The ledger tabs stay one click away, so FM speed survives. Opening a ledger turns the middle into a full paper sheet; the letter tray folds away for dense screens.
WeaknessesMap art carries the home screen, so it has to be good (a cost). It is more complex than A: three columns need care at small window sizes (the tray folds into a tab below about 1366 px wide).

All three share the top bar: guild crest, date, why the calendar stopped, and Continue. The bar holds still everywhere so the "continue, stop, deal with it" loop never moves. Names, places and numbers are placeholders.
