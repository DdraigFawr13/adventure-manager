The two-year text prototype

What you'd play: your guild for two in-game years on your phone, in sittings of 10–30 minutes. You pick contracts, send a standing party with a plan, sign recruits and answer letters. The house and staff run on defaults (D-195). Quests are told in letters home (D-197). Every adventurer keeps a dated history, a background line and friends (D-196). A death brings a memorial letter. When you pause, the page asks whom you remember (D-198).

Plain text on paper, the hub's four tabs (D-160). These screens are throwaway; the engine underneath is kept. Names, numbers and wording below are examples, not drawn from a real save.

Engine work (kept)

  • Standing parties: named, kept between quests, reshuffled per contract (D-014).
  • Histories: a person's dated entries read from events (deeds from the quest report, wounds, injuries, growth, joining, friends made).
  • Backgrounds: a home and former trade drawn on its own stream, words in content.
  • Bonds: friends after a number of quests together (a content number); flavour only.
  • Memorial letter: a template family like the quest report, with friends' reactions.

Page work (throwaway)

  • Four tabs and the pull to continue, in plain text at 15 px and up.
  • Daily autosave in the browser (Ironman, D-033); a new-game button.
  • Default player for house and staff (the simulator's policy).
  • The pause check and Copy report (D-198).
  • Built by Vercel from main for your phone (D-161, D-167).

Still open

  • How many quests together make friends (assumption: 5).
  • Who writes the memorial: the guild's chaplain or the party's leader.
  • Whether the founding draft (D-188) opens the game, or a fixed start.
