Guild portrait styles

Style Q7: how adventurers' faces look. Every recruit needs a face, and that face must age across a career, carry its scars and be remembered after death. So each style is shown on four generated adventurers, one face ageing from recruit to veteran, and a fallen hero's card. The faces are crude stand-ins built from parts in code; they show the treatment and the method, not the final art.

Method behind A, B and D: portraits are assembled from drawn parts (head, hair, brows, eyes, nose, mouth, beard, ears per ancestry, scars, age lines, kit at the collar), picked by the adventurer's seed (GAME-01) and updated by events: a scar after an injury, greying and lines with age. Parts are data, so mods can add them (D-044). Names and details are placeholders.
