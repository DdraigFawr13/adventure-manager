What you can do in it

  • Pick the approach on the start card (stealth, assault or talk), each with the best tester's chance at the barrow mouth.
  • Choice rooms: two ways, each person's chance; a failure costs the tester 4 health or warns the next room. Success at the barrow mouth catches the first fight off guard.
  • 5 or 6 rooms: the barrow mouth, 3 or 4 fights from a pool of 5, sometimes a second choice room, then the warden's burial hall.
  • Between rooms: the breather (the Down get up with 3 health; skills and the pack don't refill), Scout ahead with Wenna (seen: the next room and free row setting; heard: it is alerted).
  • Fights as in slice 1, plus Auto (a turn every 0.6 s, "Save skills for the boss" on by default, Take over) and Retreat (it asks first and shows the odds and beats).
  • The end: won, retreated or routed, with each person's state, injuries, and the monster parts found.

Balance (1,000 seeded delves each, measured)

  • Two numbers changed, both content: the chance a Down ally starts bleeding out each round, 3,000 → 1,095 basis points (gate 1's own; the year simulator keeps 3,000), and the warden's health, 40 → 34. Before: about 38% of careful delves cost a life, nearly all in the warden's room.
  • The careful player now loses a life in 9.9% of delves (95% interval 8.1–11.8), inside the spec's 8–12%. Wins 76.1%, retreats 22.9%, routed 1.0%; fights take 4.19 rounds on average; no fight stalls. Auto in every fight: wins 93.2%, routed 6.8%, loses a life in 27.1% of delves.
  • Note: at 1,000 delves the interval is about 3.7 points wide and the band is 4, so this check only passes when the middle sits within about 0.2 points of 10%. Small future changes will flip it to "near".
