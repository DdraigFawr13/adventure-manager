How the game tells you why

One consequence, told three ways (PLAN §8c, Q8a). Names and numbers are examples.

Week 6: you decline the crown's contract to clear Hollow Barrow→ Week 9: rumours of the dead walking at night→ Week 14: they take the Ashford road→ Ashford standing −8, trade income −10%

A · Plain ledgers, causes spelled out

A short notice, and the full chain lives in the inspector: every value has a dated history and each entry says what caused it.

Notice week 14

The Ashford road is closed. The dead from Hollow Barrow attacked a carter's train at dusk.

Caused by: you declined "Clear the barrow at Hollow Barrow" (week 6).

B · In-world only

The world tells you, in its own voice. People blame you when they would; the ledger shows the new numbers but not the chain.

From the Reeve of Ashford week 14

You will have heard. Three carts burned on the Ashford road, and old Jory Tamm is dead. The crown's men say they offered your company that barrow two months gone, and you would not take it.

I do not say it was your fault. I say Ashford will remember who would not come.

Garret Lowe, Reeve of Ashford

C · In-world first, with a trace Recommended

The same letter, plus a small "Trace" mark on it and on every changed value. Trace opens the plain chain of causes, each step dated and linked, with what the guild knew at each point.
