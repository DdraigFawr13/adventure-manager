The first season

Three ways to teach the game in its first in-game year (PLAN §6a). The same guild, The Lantern Company, in week 3 of a founded save. Names and numbers are examples.

A · Staff open the ledgers Recommended

Ledgers start sealed and open one by one when they first matter: the first injury opens the Infirmary, the first wages bill opens Finances. A staff member writes a short letter explaining the new ledger. A "veteran start" setting opens everything on day one.

Settings → Veteran start opens all ledgers at once.

GoodEach system arrives with a reason and a person. Matches the "gentle first season" (D-022) and the staff-led delegation the game teaches anyway.
CostEach ledger needs an opening trigger and a letter; a fallback date opens it if the trigger never fires.
VeteransOne setting skips it. A veteran who skips loses nothing but the letters.

B · Everything open, clerk's notes in the margins

All ledgers are open from day one, as in FM. The first time a sheet opens, the guild clerk has pencilled two or three notes on it pointing at what matters. Notes can be put away for good.

C · A founding prologue, then the full game

A short scripted first contract (about 30 minutes): a fixed party clears a mill cellar while a checklist walks through briefing, watching the board and a key moment. Then the full game opens.
