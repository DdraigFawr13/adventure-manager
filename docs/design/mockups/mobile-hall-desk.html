Hall and Road: the work desk

Your version: the guildhall is the hub, every room does its own job, and the satchel becomes a work desk for everything that spans the whole guild: notices, contracts, the full roster and the year's numbers.

Built on the Hall and Road concept. The four tabs are now Hall, March (the world map), Desk and Ask. The desk also sits in the hall as your study, so you can reach it either way. Each room shows its staff member and whether you or they run it (CONCEPT §19); the few things never delegated (D-038) say so. Hours are estimates (REP-01).

The hub and the desk

The hall tells you where to look: a red number on a room means something there needs you. The desk is where you go to see the guild as a whole.

The March: the world map

The hall is inside; the March is outside. The second tab (or the gate) opens the kingdom map from D-134. Pinch to zoom from the whole March down to a town; layers switch what the map shows. Tap a contract seal, a party or a rival's crest for its sheet. A party on a quest opens the quest board (STYLE §4), drawn in ink as they explore.

What this changes, and the work

Better than the satchel

  • One clear rule: the room for one system, the desk for the whole guild.
  • Deep numbers and comparisons live on the desk, so Pillar 5's depth is never far away.
  • Delegation becomes visible: each room shows who runs it.

Watch out for

  • Two ways to the same person (barracks and desk roster); both must open the same person card.
  • Twelve rooms is a lot to draw; the hall can start with the launch rooms and grow with upgrades.
  • The sentence-style brief from the satchel can live on in the contract briefing (+60–120 h), or go.
