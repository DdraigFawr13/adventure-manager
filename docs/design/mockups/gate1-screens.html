Gate 1 screens

Gate 0 mock-ups of four screens the first delve needs (D-214): the fight, the party and its gear, the smith, and the weekly budget. Each frame is 375 px wide, the first phone width (D-159). One timeline runs through them: the Fen Lanterns delve into the Hollow Barrow in week 6 and refit at home in week 7.

Dotted numbers and lines are starting ideas or stand-ins; the gate 1 spec sets them (D-218, D-221, D-222). Plain numbers come from the content files or a decision; the check record at the end lists where each comes from. Maud Fenwick is a stand-in: the content has no wizard yet (D-219 adds the class), so her name is drawn from the name pools in naming.json.

1. The fight

Foes at the top, the party's front row under them, the back row next, and the acting adventurer's skills in a tray at the bottom where a thumb reaches. The turn strip shows this round's order by speed. Each foe shows its intent; once a skill is picked, each target shows the hit chance and damage range before the tap (D-218).
