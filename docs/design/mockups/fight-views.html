Fight camera: three options

The owner asked for a side-on fight: the party on the left, the foes on the right, the camera a little above so the rows have depth, and a bar at the bottom for actions and health. All three show the same moment as the approved mock-up (D-224): room 3 of 6, round 2, Wenna's turn, Strike from shadow aimed at Hound 2, which Odric already hit this round. Front rows stand nearest the middle and back rows behind; pieces further away stand higher and smaller.

Dotted numbers and lines are starting ideas or stand-ins the gate 1 spec sets. The painted hall behind is the existing backdrop, which faces the far wall; a side-on battle backdrop would be new art. Maud Fenwick is a stand-in wizard (no wizard in content yet).
