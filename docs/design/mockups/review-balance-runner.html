What it does

node tools/balance/run.ts house-incidents plays the scenario's 100 seeded careers and prints about 30 measures, each with a 95% interval and a verdict against its target and source decision. --set tries a number without editing files, --sweep tries several in one run, and --compare pairs every career with stored results from main, saying how many are identical. Usage and limits: tools/balance/README.md.

What the runner found in existing numbers

  • Incidents' "12% dismissed against 14% without" is within the margin: the change is −2 points, interval −8 to +4.
  • Incidents do raise bad starts: +9 points, interval +2 to +16.
  • 20 careers is too few for how careers end (±20 points on dismissals) but suits a first look at rates a year.
  • The other small recorded gaps (13% / 14% / 15% by starter party) are also within the margin. Re-measuring them is a question on the roadmap.

  • Major
    Nothing tied a baseline to the content it was played on; seeds weren't checked when pairing.Fixed: baselines keep a content hash and the run warns when content differs (checked by changing one number); seeds are checked; a baseline commit outside this branch is flagged. Baselines re-saved; still 100 of 100 identical.
