Desaturated and heavy; leans into permadeath. Strong mood, but low contrast makes status colours harder to tell apart, and hours in it can feel draining.

Whichever mood wins, two rules hold: status colours (good, warning, bad) stay a fixed, colour-blind-safe set separate from the mood, and faction colours get their own heraldic set. The mood sets the desk, paper, ink and accents only. Names, classes and data here are placeholders.
