Mock-up · round 2 · D-168–D-184

The guild house and its town on the phone

Seven phone screens: a new guild's hall, the same hall six years on, the compound to either side, building a room, a room's own page, and the home town as a map.

Round 2 shows your answers of 2026-10-27 (listed at the end): the compound beside the hall with four outdoor-only buildings, the town as a top-down map with plots, no signing without a bed, upkeep, and the town as a faction. Every cost, week count, bed count and percentage on these phones is still a placeholder for the year simulator to set.

1 · A new guild

The hall opens on the hall tab as before (D-155). It starts with the study and its work desk, the common room and a small barracks. The gate has moved out to the compound wall (D-180), so the ground floor has a free cell. Swipe left or right, or tap the yard tabs at the edges, to walk out into the compound.

What you do here

  • Tap a room to open it (screen 5).
  • Tap a dashed cell to build (screen 4).
  • "Raise a storey" adds 3 cells on top; "Dig a cellar" adds 3 below. Each costs more than the last (D-172).

Placeholders

  • 6 beds, 120 g and 3 weeks for a cellar.
  • Whether building shows always or only in a Build mode (the chip in the top bar).

2 · The same hall, six years on

The seven hall kinds are built. Three cells of barracks side by side have merged into one room of 24 beds, and it is full, so no one can be signed until there is a bed (D-174). The infirmary is full too, so its third cell is being built. Roman numerals are room levels.

What it shows

  • Merging: 3 cells of one kind read as one room with one head.
  • Pressure at a glance: full rooms are tinted.
  • The strongroom below ground, the one hall room locked to a floor (D-176, D-180).

Placeholders

  • The pace: 3 storeys and a cellar by year 6; the real pace comes from the costs (D-172).

3 · The compound

Your addition. The compound continues the hall's picture at ground level, in the same side-on view (D-180). The west yard holds the gate in the wall and a training yard two plots wide; the east yard holds the forge, the works yard and a free plot. These four kinds can only be built out here (D-178, D-180).

Decided, shown

  • Side-on, reached by swiping; the hall stays home (D-155, D-180).
  • Gate, training yard, works yard and forge are compound-only.
  • Where a kind may go is a field in its data (GAME-04).

Assumed, not decided

  • 3 plots a side, so 6 in all, with walls that can be pushed out later.
  • Plots follow the hall's rules: 1–3 wide, same kinds merge, 3 levels.

4 · Building a room

Tap a dashed cell and a sheet rises with the kinds that can go there, their cost, weeks and weekly upkeep (D-177). The top line is what suits this cell best: here the infirmary next door, which the new cell would join. Kinds that can't go here are greyed out with the reason (D-176). Compound kinds are listed only when you build in the yard (D-176).

Decided, shown

  • Builds take weeks, best in winter (D-067, D-169).
  • Merging with a same-kind neighbour (D-169).
  • Upkeep on every room (D-177); floor locks with reasons (D-176).

Placeholders

  • All costs, weeks and upkeep; whether a winter build is cheaper, faster or only safer.

5 · A room's own page

Capacity comes first: six beds, all full, and Kit healing at the slow rate because there is no bed. The head's skill turns into a plain effect. The policy chips are how the head fills the beds, so you never have to place anyone by hand (D-170); "I choose" is there if you want to.

Decided, shown

  • Size and level set capacity; one head sets strength (D-170).
  • Placing by policy, by hand optional (D-022, D-170).
  • 3 levels (D-172) and upkeep (D-177).

Placeholders

  • 1.4× healing, 9 g a week, 400 g and 5 weeks for level III.

6 · The home town

Your change: the town is a top-down map you scroll and pinch, like the March but closer in (D-179). It is one hand-made town, the same in every game (D-184). The 5 sites stand from the start with their levels shown; the market is still unfunded, and the guild's first payment founds it. The 3 dashed plots are held for site kinds added in updates (D-179, D-184). The bar at the top is the town's faction standing, with the mark below which the boosts stop (D-175). Tap a site to open its sheet; here the temple, which a rival is trying to buy.

Decided, shown

  • Top-down, scrollable, plots and places marked (D-179).
  • 5 sites standing, 3 spare plots; townsfolk own the sites (D-171, D-184).
  • Standing is the town's faction standing; the town is one of the two cities (D-175, D-181).

Placeholders

  • The drawn layout, and what the spare plots show before an update fills them.
  • Funding as a monthly payment rather than one-off gifts.
  • The red mark at 50 and every price.

Your answers, 2026-10-27

  1. Rooms: 3 levels each; cells cost more the higher or deeper they are; a full house is about 8–12 years of a mid guild's surplus. D-172
  2. Incidents: a few a year, as letters with a choice. D-173
  3. Full barracks: no bed, no signing. D-174
  4. Town standing: the town is a faction. D-175
  5. Floor locks: the strongroom below ground; the gate moved to the compound. D-176, D-180
  6. Upkeep: a small weekly cost per room. D-177
  7. Compound: side-on beside the hall; the gate, training yard, works yard and forge are compound-only. D-178, D-180
  8. Town map: top-down and scrollable, with plots where the guild founds sites by patronage. D-179
  9. Later answers: the home town is one of the two city factions; members who lose their beds lodge at the inn for a fee; the compound starts at 3 plots a side and can be pushed out; one hand-made town with 5 sites and 3 spare plots. D-181–D-184
